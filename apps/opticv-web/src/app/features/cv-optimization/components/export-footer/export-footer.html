All templates are ATS safe.

All templates follow ATS best practices:

  • Single-column layout - no sidebars or tables
  • Standard section headings (Professional Summary, Work Experience, Education, Skills, Languages)
  • Clean heading hierarchy
  • No images, icons, or graphics, just pure text
  • Standard fonts

Use the Accent Color select (in the export section) to try different accent colors: emerald, blue, purple, red, or teal, which updates the Default, Modern, Corporate, and Impact templates dynamically.
