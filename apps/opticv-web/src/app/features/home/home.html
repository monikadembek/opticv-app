How to use OptiCV

From upload to optimized CV in 3 simple steps.

1. Upload your CV

Import your existing CV once (PDF or DOCX format). We parse it and reuse it across every feature in this app. * Currently we support English only CVs

2. Paste job description

Paste job posting and run optimization process - OptiCV goes to work: scoring, rewriting, and flagging gaps against that specific role, not a generic template.

3. Get your optimized CV

Review AI suggestions, apply changes or edit inline, then export your tailored, ATS friendly CV, along with a cover letter, interview prep and information to put on your Linkedin profile page.
