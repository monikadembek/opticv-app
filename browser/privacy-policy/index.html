This Privacy Policy explains how OptiCV (“OptiCV”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal data when you use the OptiCV web application at app.opticv.net and the website at opticv.net (together, the “Service”). It applies to all users, wherever they live. If you live in the European Union (EU), the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom (UK), the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) or the UK GDPR gives you the rights described in section 9.

2. What data we collect

2.1 Data you give us

  • Account data: your email address, and the display name you choose (optional).
  • CVs and documents: the CV files you upload (PDF or DOCX), the text and structured data we extract from them, the job descriptions you paste or upload, and everything the Service generates for you: optimized CVs, cover letters, interview preparation, keyword analysis, ATS scores and LinkedIn content.
  • Email preferences: whether you want product updates and job-search tips by email.
  • Correspondence: the content of the emails you send us, for example support requests, complaints or withdrawal notices.

A note about what you put in your CV. A CV naturally contains personal data such as your work history, education and contact details. Please don't include information you don't need for the job application, especially sensitive information such as health data, religious beliefs, political opinions, ethnic origin, sexual orientation, trade union membership or ID numbers. If your CV contains such information, we only process it as part of your document, to provide the Service you asked for. If your CV includes other people's data (for example a referee's contact details), make sure you are allowed to share it.

2.2 Data created when you use the Service

  • Subscription data: your plan (Free, Basic or Pro), its status, your billing period and the identifiers Stripe gives your customer account and subscription. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe handles payment details.
  • Usage data: which features you use and how often (for example the number of optimizations in a billing period), so we can apply your plan's limits.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages visited, timestamps, server logs and error reports.
  • Analytics data: how you interact with the Service (pages viewed, buttons clicked), collected with PostHog in the app and with Google Analytics on our website (opticv.net). See section 11.
  • Session replays: for a sample of sessions, and for sessions where an error happens, our error-monitoring tool (Sentry) records a reconstruction of what happened on the page so we can fix bugs. By default, Sentry masks all text and form inputs and blocks images and media, so replays don't show your CV content or what you type.

4. How we use AI

To optimize your CV and generate related content, we send the relevant text (your CV content and the job description) to OpenAI through its API. Under OpenAI's API terms, OpenAI does not use this data to train its models. It may keep it for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, then deletes it. We do not use your CVs or generated content to train any AI model.

AI output is a suggestion for you to review. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you (Art. 22 GDPR). The ATS score is an estimate that helps you compare versions of your CV. It is not a decision about you.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account data, CVs and generated content: for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual CVs and job applications at any time. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your account data, CV files and generated content straight away. Copies may remain in our encrypted backups for up to 30 days, until those backups are overwritten.
  • Inactive accounts: if you don't sign in for 24 months, we may delete your account and data. We'll email you at least 30 days before doing so, and signing in will keep your account.
  • Payment and invoice records: Stripe keeps them, and we keep them as long as tax and accounting law requires, which in Poland is generally 5 years from the end of the year in which the payment was made.
  • Logs, error reports and session replays: up to 30 days.
  • Analytics data: up to 12 months in PostHog, and up to 14 months in Google Analytics.
  • Correspondence, complaints and withdrawal notices: as long as needed to deal with them, and after that for as long as legal claims could arise (generally up to 6 years under Polish law).

8. How we protect your data

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest provided by our hosting and storage providers, passwordless sign-in, access controls, row-level security in our database, and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at high risk, we will tell you, as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • erase your data. You can delete your account yourself in Settings;
  • restrict how we process your data;
  • data portability: receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format. You can also download your generated documents directly in the app;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time. This doesn't affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these rights, email opticvapp@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first, usually by asking you to write from the email address on your account.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland, this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl). You can also complain to the authority where you live or work. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
